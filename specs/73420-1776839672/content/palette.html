E16 — Zerg Color Palette

What should Zerg bodies look like? The teamColor decal goes on top (blue for friendly, red for enemy).

A — Classic Purple Organic

Dark purple carapace, fleshy purple undertones, glowing yellow eyes. The canonical SC2 Zerg look.

B — Black Chitin / Green Bio ★ recommended

Near-black chitinous shell, toxic green bio-fluid accents, acid-green eyes. Menacing, high contrast, reads well at sprite scale.

C — Brown Organic / Purple Highlights

Warm brown carapace, purple-tinted flesh in shadows, amber eyes. More naturalistic, earthy feel.
