Purple body, yellow eyes already decided. Where does teamColor (blue/red) appear?

Dorsal spines and frills glow in teamColor. Zergling spikes, Hydralisk fin-spines, Mutalisk wing-tips.

Organic glands and sacs pulse with teamColor bio-fluid. Roach acid glands, Hydralisk throat sac, Zergling belly gland. Distinctly Zerg, easily read.

Rim lighting on carapace edges in teamColor. Subtle — works at large sizes, may be hard to read at sprite scale.
